What we do

Service specification

Every job starts with a proper measure-up. Here's what's typically on the sheet.

TIL

Tiling & Floors

Kitchens, passages, sculleries and bathrooms — removal of old tiles and laying new, including splashbacks and edge finishing.

PLM

Plumbing & Drainage

New plumbing for showers and baths, basin relocation, and drainage lines trenched and laid to fall.

GLZ

Doors & Glazing

Aluminium bifold doors, sliding doors and shutters — supplied and fitted, from opening prep through to final glazing.

PAV

Paving & Driveways

Interlocking paver driveways and courtyards, bedded and laid to proper falls for drainage.

DEM

Demolition & Strip-outs

Shower wall and slab demolition, bath tub removal, old tile stripping — cleared properly before the rebuild starts.

WPF

Waterproofing & Ceilings

Rhinolite and paint ceilings above showers, new shower traps and mosaic floor installations, cornicing and finishing.

CRP

Carpentry & Roofing

Roof trusses and ceiling battens, kick plates, skirting and fixed fittings — structural and finishing carpentry.

INT

Interior Finishes & Painting

Wall finishing and painting for renovated or newly built spaces, inside homes and commercial fit-outs alike.

GEN

General Maintenance

Smaller repair and upkeep jobs around the home or business that don't need a full renovation team.

How it works

From measure-up to move back in

  1. 01

    Site measure & quote

    We come out, measure every surface that's actually being worked on, and price it against those real numbers — not a rough estimate.

  2. 02

    Schedule & strip-out

    Work is sequenced properly — demolition and plumbing first, then tiling and finishing — so nothing gets redone.

  3. 03

    Finish & sign-off

    Final fittings, cornicing and paintwork go in last. You walk the site with us before we call it done.
